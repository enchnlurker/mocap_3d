Stage 2c
- Lower bound only for joints within 12 cm of a hand centre and for the nearer end of an impossible bone.
- Depth interpolated between samples; covered at either sample = covered. Interpolated rows share the samples' total weight: at full weight they cost 0.3 cm MPJPE on the synthetic test, with shared weight they gain 0.2 cm.
- Shoulder turn rate may change ≤ 0.6°/frame² while a shoulder's depth is hidden. A cap on the turn itself held back dance01's spin and made it jump 13°/frame to catch up; capping the rate change lets spins through and still stops a sudden reversal.
- Shoulder/hip width from the image (p95 of the low-passed on-screen span at the fit's own distance, ±4 %) when it reaches 0.75 of 2b's width. A raw p95 read 4–6 % wide from 2D jitter, and a width fixed in metres at the rigid pose's scale fought the depth-driven scale; both fixed. sit01's shoulders: 0.36 m from the image vs 0.25 m from ratios.
- Side-view limits: hip adduction ≤ 15°, knee hinge ≤ 35° off the hip line (faded out below 10–30° of bend: near straight the hinge is noise, and that cost 0.4 cm on the synthetic walk), pelvis roll ≤ 12°, upright ankle midpoint ≤ 5 cm sideways.
- Synthetic MPJPE with depth 3.0 → 2.8 cm; without depth 10.5 → 10.3 cm.

## Correctness fixes and retuning

Fixes
- 2c lap rule: a hip's own thigh never counted as covering it, so a seated hip read the lap. Now hip + pelvis are lower bounds when the thigh points ≥ 30° at the camera.
- 2c skin depth: per-joint offset added to each reading (hips 8 cm … wrists 2.5 cm); relative depth cancels a common offset, not these.

Tuning (synthetic MPJPE with depth stays 2.8 cm, without 10.4 cm)
- 2D σ = 1 % of the shorter image side, points weighted by visibility (0.2–1).
- 2b prior quadratic: σ 1.0 limbs, 2.0 torso. σ 0.5 / 1.0 was tried: same shake, 2D error up to 5.9 px on dance01, synthetic 3.6 cm.
- Smoothness quadratic σ 0.012 m/frame². σ 0.006 penalised real walking (the true synthetic pose cost 9× the wrong fit; no-depth error 10 → 26 cm); walking wrists/ankles reach ~0.01 m/frame².
- 2b: height clamped to 1.50–1.95 m (sit01 1.38 → 1.50); head ≤ 20° off the spine. Head ratio stays 0.115: at 0.092 the head hit the +12 % length limit on every clip.
- Retarget: ends 3, heels 0.3, neutral pull 0.3, 40 iterations, pops counted at 15°. New bend pull on elbows/knees toward the angle their three 2c points measure: dance01's left elbow collapsed straight and was unstuck 29 times.
